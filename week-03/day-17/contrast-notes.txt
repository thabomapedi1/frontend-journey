PART 5: QUIZ

1. What does a ratio of 21:1 mean?

It means there is the highest possible contrast ratio between two colours, such as black text on a white background.
2. What ratio does AA need?
Normal text: 4.5:1.
Large text: 3:1.

3. A form shows required fields only in red. What's the problem, and how would you fix it?

Some users may not be able to distinguish the red colour, so I would also add text such as "Required field" to communicate the information.

4. Why does contrast matter on a phone?
Good contrast makes text easier to read on small screens, particularly in bright sunlight or when users have visual difficulties.
